How we work

Five stages.
Zero guesswork.

Most agencies wing it. We don’t. Every engagement follows the same five-stage framework — because the same data-led system that works for a $5k/mo account works for a $500k/mo account. The scale changes. The discipline doesn’t.

01

Audit

Five days. Every dollar accounted for.

Before we touch anything, we map your entire setup. Every ad account, every pixel, every conversion event, every dollar of spend — traced back to actual revenue. Not impressions, not clicks. Revenue.

Most accounts we audit have at least three things bleeding money silently: misattributed conversions, duplicate audiences, and ad sets that look fine in the dashboard but haven’t generated a single profitable sale in 60 days.

Deliverable Full account audit report — spend breakdown, waste identified, quick wins, 90-day opportunity map
02

Strategy

Written down. Approved. No surprises.

Based on the audit, we build a full channel strategy: which platforms get budget and why, how it’s allocated, what KPIs we’re optimizing toward, creative direction, and audience architecture. You see it before we build anything.

This is where most agencies skip straight to launching campaigns. We don’t. A week of planning prevents three months of wasted spend.

Deliverable Channel strategy document — budget allocation, KPI targets, creative brief, audience map, 30/60/90-day milestones
03

Build

Campaigns built from first principles.

We build or rebuild campaigns from scratch based on the approved strategy. Account structure, campaign naming conventions, ad copy, creative assets, landing page alignment, conversion tracking — everything owned and documented.

Nothing is assumed to work. Every targeting decision has a data rationale. Every ad has a hypothesis. Every landing page is matched to the ad’s intent signal.

Deliverable Live campaigns, verified tracking, creative library, account structure documentation
04

Launch & Iterate

Controlled ramp. Weekly optimization cadence.

We don’t go full-budget on day one. Spend ramps in controlled phases while we watch every signal — CTR, CPM, add-to-cart rate, conversion rate, ROAS. The first 14 days are the most important. That’s when the algorithm learns and when bad assumptions surface.

After launch, we run a weekly optimization cycle: bid adjustments, creative rotation, audience pruning, landing page updates. No coasting. No “let’s see how it performs this month.”

Cadence Weekly optimization reviews + Slack updates on significant changes
05

Report

Dollars, not dashboards.

Monthly reporting in revenue impact. Not impressions, not reach, not CTR. We report on what you actually care about: how much you spent, how much you made, what the ROAS was, what CPL looked like, and what changed from last month.

Every report includes: what we learned, what we’re changing, and what we’re testing next. No inflated percentages. No “up to” claims. Just what actually happened.

Deliverable Monthly revenue report — spend, ROAS, CPL, MoM comparison, next 30-day plan
Common questions

What people ask
before they work with us.

How long does the audit take?
Five business days from account access. You get a full written report, not a sales call with a slide deck.
Do you require long-term contracts?
Initial engagements are 3 months minimum — enough time to run the full five-stage process and show real results. After that, month-to-month.
What’s the minimum monthly ad spend?
$5,000/month in managed ad spend. Below that, the optimization lever isn’t big enough to move meaningful numbers.
Do you work with accounts in bad shape?
Yes — and those often produce the biggest wins. Messy accounts with wasted spend have the most room to improve. Clean accounts are harder to move.
What verticals do you work in?
B2B SaaS, D2C ecommerce, commercial insurance, healthcare, real estate, and professional services. If your business has a measurable conversion event and enough ad spend to work with, we can help.
