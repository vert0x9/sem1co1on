The situation

An apparel brand doing $2M+ in annual revenue came to us after 18 months of flat Meta performance. They were spending $100k over 90 days and breaking even at best. ROAS had been hovering around 1.4× for two quarters. Their agency told them the account was "optimized." It wasn't.

What we found in the audit

  • 67% of spend was concentrated in 2 ad sets that hadn't generated a profitable sale in 45 days
  • Audience overlap between retargeting and prospecting campaigns was above 60% — they were paying to show the same ad to the same people twice
  • No creative testing framework — the same 3 static images had been running for 4 months
  • Landing page mismatch: ads promised "free shipping on orders over $75," landing page had no mention of the offer
  • Conversion tracking double-counting view-through and click-through — reported ROAS was 1.4×, actual was closer to 0.9×

What we did

01
Killed the waste. Paused the two underperforming ad sets immediately. Reallocated that budget to three new audience segments: high-intent purchasers (30-day website visitors, add-to-cart not purchased), lookalikes from top 5% customer LTV list, and interest-based prospecting with tighter demographic filters.
02
Rebuilt the funnel structure. Separated campaigns into TOF (prospecting), MOF (engagement), and BOF (retargeting) with distinct budgets, objectives, and creative. No audience overlap above 15%.
03
Introduced UGC creative testing framework. Sourced 12 UGC video clips from existing customers. Built a 3:2:1 testing ratio per ad set — 3 video variants, 2 static image variants, 1 carousel. Rotated creative every 14 days based on CPA performance.
04
Fixed tracking and landing page. Rebuilt the Meta pixel with server-side events via Conversions API — eliminated double-counting. Updated landing pages to match ad copy promises with consistent offer messaging.
05
Weekly optimization cadence. Bid adjustments every Tuesday. Creative performance review every two weeks. Audience refresh monthly. No week without a documented change.
