The situation

An HR tech SaaS company running $18k/month in Google Ads came to us with a CPL problem. They were paying $340 per MQL, their sales team was closing 8% of those leads, and the unit economics barely worked. Two previous agencies had "optimized" the account. The account wasn't optimized — it was patched.

What we found in the audit

  • 340 low-intent, irrelevant keywords in broad and phrase match — accounting for $6,200/month in clicks that never converted
  • Brand and non-brand campaigns sharing the same budget pool — brand was cannibalizing non-brand budget
  • Ad copy using feature-based headlines ("Automate HR workflows") with no pain-point framing
  • Landing page headline didn't match any of the ad copy — Quality Scores averaging 4/10
  • Conversion tracking set up with both GA4 goals and Google Ads goals active — 40% of conversions were being counted twice

What we did

01
Keyword audit and purge. Exported full search term report for the prior 6 months. Built negative keyword list of 340 terms — job seekers, students, irrelevant industries. Added 127 new high-intent long-tail keywords missed entirely by the previous setup.
02
Rebuilt campaign architecture. Separated brand vs. non-brand into distinct campaigns with independent budgets. Split non-brand into top-of-funnel (awareness keywords) and bottom-of-funnel (demo/trial-intent keywords) with different bid strategies and landing pages.
03
Ad copy A/B test: pain-point vs. feature. Ran pain-point headlines ("Still tracking employee hours in spreadsheets?") against feature-based variants. Pain-point won — 31% higher CTR, 22% lower CPC. Rolled out across all ad groups.
04
Landing page alignment. Updated each campaign's landing page to mirror its ad copy messaging. Quality Score went from an average of 4/10 to 8/10 across non-brand campaigns — CPC dropped 28% without changing bids.
05
Fixed conversion tracking. Consolidated to a single conversion source — Google Ads tags via GTM, server-side confirmed. Removed the GA4 dual-tracking setup. Actual CPL was $340; after fixing tracking, the pre-optimization CPL was revealed to be $194. We had more room to improve than the client thought.
