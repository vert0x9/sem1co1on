The situation

A commercial insurance broker in the Northeast was spending $12k/month across Meta and Google and generating 18 qualified leads per month at a CPL of $667. They'd been running the same campaigns for 14 months. Performance had plateaued, and the broker assumed the market was just "getting harder." The market wasn't the problem.

What we found in the audit

  • $8k/month going to Google, $4k to Meta — but the data showed Meta CPL was 60% lower for equivalent lead quality
  • Google campaigns targeting broad insurance keywords with massive competition and no negative keyword list
  • Meta audience was a single interest-based set — no lookalikes, no CRM-based targeting
  • Landing page had 9 form fields including "Best time to call" and "How did you hear about us" — both before any trust was established
  • Zero retargeting — every visitor who didn't convert on the first visit was gone forever
  • No social proof on the landing page — no reviews, no credentials, no trust indicators

What we did

01
Flipped the budget allocation. Moved $5k from Google to Meta based on CPL data — new split was $3k Google, $9k Meta. Reduced Google spend to branded and high-intent transactional terms only ("commercial liability insurance quote").
02
Rebuilt Meta audience architecture. Uploaded 3 years of CRM data (past policyholders). Built lookalike audiences at 1%, 2%, and 5% from the highest-LTV customer segment. Layered income and industry targeting on top. Removed all broad interest-based targeting.
03
Landing page overhaul. Cut form fields from 9 to 4 (name, email, phone, coverage type). Added BBB badge, 200+ Google review count, and three client testimonials above the fold. Wrote new headline: "Get your commercial insurance quote in 24 hours" — clear, specific, no jargon.
04
Built a 3-stage retargeting sequence. Stage 1 (days 1–7): awareness video showing the broker's 20-year track record. Stage 2 (days 8–30): educational content about what commercial coverage actually covers. Stage 3 (days 31–60): direct quote offer with urgency. Each stage had unique creative — no repeated ads.
05
Lead quality tracking. Worked with the broker's CRM to tag lead source at the deal level. Within 60 days we could see which audiences produced leads that actually closed — and doubled down on those.
