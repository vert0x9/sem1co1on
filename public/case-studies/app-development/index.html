The projects

Two separate products. Same discipline: start with the problem, not the solution. Both apps serve the NYC construction and real estate industry — an industry that runs on paper, spreadsheets, and missed DOB notifications. We set out to fix that.

Salutem: Construction Logs

View on App Store →

NYC construction superintendents log everything by hand — daily reports, safety checks, accident reports, hot work authorizations. The paper trail is mandatory, the process is brutal. Salutem replaces paper with a centralized digital system built for how supers actually work on site.

Problem
Paper-based compliance logging in a heavily regulated industry. Supers filing the same report types daily with no digital record, no audit trail, and no way to share updates with PMs in real time.
Product
Daily log digitization, role-based team access, automated PDF report generation via email, certificate tracking, and NYC DOB compliance support. Built for the job site — not the office.
Outcome
Live on the App Store under ILAYBRICK CORP. Rated 5/5 stars. Users describe it as "finally bringing construction management into the modern age."

Citiscape: NYC DOB Alerts

View on App Store →

In NYC construction, a DOB violation you didn't know about becomes a court date. A missed permit expiration becomes a stop-work order. Citiscape gives developers, contractors, and property managers real-time visibility into everything the Department of Buildings is doing — before it becomes a problem.

Problem
NYC developers were checking DOB's public portal manually, missing violations, complaints, and permit expirations until they received physical notice — often weeks too late to respond cheaply.
Product
Instant push alerts for DOB violations, complaints, permits, and inspections. Team messaging organized by project, cloud document storage, calendar integration for permit expiration, CitiWatch community crowdsourcing, and CITI-GPT — an AI assistant for construction compliance questions.
Outcome
Live on the App Store. Serving active construction teams across NYC. First of its kind AI-integrated DOB monitoring platform.

What the full lifecycle looked like

01
Problem validation. Interviews with NYC supers and developers. Mapped the pain points against what existing tools missed. Both products found clear unserved demand before a line of code was written.
02
Product strategy. Feature prioritization for MVP. What had to be in v1. What could wait. Defined the user journey, core flows, and success metrics before design started.
03
Development oversight. Managed the full development cycle — sprint planning, QA, revision cycles, App Store submission. Both apps passed App Review on first submission.
04
App Store launch and GTM. App Store optimization (title, keywords, screenshots, description). Initial user acquisition strategy. Feedback loops to inform v2 roadmap.
