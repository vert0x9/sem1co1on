<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">The projects</h2>
<p>Two separate products. Same discipline: start with the problem, not the solution. Both apps serve the NYC construction and real estate industry — an industry that runs on paper, spreadsheets, and missed DOB notifications. We set out to fix that.</p>
</div>

<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">Salutem: Construction Logs</h2>
<p class="cs-app-sub"><a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/salutem-construction-logs/id1499496815" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="cs-app-link">View on App Store &rarr;</a></p>
<p>NYC construction superintendents log everything by hand — daily reports, safety checks, accident reports, hot work authorizations. The paper trail is mandatory, the process is brutal. Salutem replaces paper with a centralized digital system built for how supers actually work on site.</p>
<div class="cs-steps">
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">Problem</span>
    <div>Paper-based compliance logging in a heavily regulated industry. Supers filing the same report types daily with no digital record, no audit trail, and no way to share updates with PMs in real time.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">Product</span>
    <div>Daily log digitization, role-based team access, automated PDF report generation via email, certificate tracking, and NYC DOB compliance support. Built for the job site — not the office.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">Outcome</span>
    <div>Live on the App Store under ILAYBRICK CORP. Rated 5/5 stars. Users describe it as "finally bringing construction management into the modern age."</div>
  </div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">Citiscape: NYC DOB Alerts</h2>
<p class="cs-app-sub"><a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/citiscape-nyc-dob-alerts/id1418119077" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="cs-app-link">View on App Store &rarr;</a></p>
<p>In NYC construction, a DOB violation you didn't know about becomes a court date. A missed permit expiration becomes a stop-work order. Citiscape gives developers, contractors, and property managers real-time visibility into everything the Department of Buildings is doing — before it becomes a problem.</p>
<div class="cs-steps">
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">Problem</span>
    <div>NYC developers were checking DOB's public portal manually, missing violations, complaints, and permit expirations until they received physical notice — often weeks too late to respond cheaply.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">Product</span>
    <div>Instant push alerts for DOB violations, complaints, permits, and inspections. Team messaging organized by project, cloud document storage, calendar integration for permit expiration, CitiWatch community crowdsourcing, and CITI-GPT — an AI assistant for construction compliance questions.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">Outcome</span>
    <div>Live on the App Store. Serving active construction teams across NYC. First of its kind AI-integrated DOB monitoring platform.</div>
  </div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">What the full lifecycle looked like</h2>
<div class="cs-steps">
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">01</span>
    <div><strong>Problem validation.</strong> Interviews with NYC supers and developers. Mapped the pain points against what existing tools missed. Both products found clear unserved demand before a line of code was written.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">02</span>
    <div><strong>Product strategy.</strong> Feature prioritization for MVP. What had to be in v1. What could wait. Defined the user journey, core flows, and success metrics before design started.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">03</span>
    <div><strong>Development oversight.</strong> Managed the full development cycle — sprint planning, QA, revision cycles, App Store submission. Both apps passed App Review on first submission.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">04</span>
    <div><strong>App Store launch and GTM.</strong> App Store optimization (title, keywords, screenshots, description). Initial user acquisition strategy. Feedback loops to inform v2 roadmap.</div>
  </div>
</div>
