<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">The situation</h2>
<p>An online ecommerce store selling niche photography equipment needed a clearer view of how its advertising, product catalog, and search presence worked together. We started on May 11, 2026, with an audit of Google Ads and the store's organic search setup. We compared the four full months before the engagement, January through April, with the four full months after it began, June through September.</p>
</div>

<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">What we found in the audit</h2>
<ul class="cs-list">
  <li>Product and service intent were mixed in the Google Ads account, making it harder to assess the value of different searches.</li>
  <li>The product feed had gaps, while the initial campaign plan called for clearer catalog and margin segmentation.</li>
  <li>Search visibility needed work across product collections, structured data, and content.</li>
  <li>Attribution needed attention: Shopify and ad-platform reports did not always agree on which channel deserved credit for a sale.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">What we did</h2>
<div class="cs-steps">
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">01</span>
    <div><strong>Audited the account and mapped a plan.</strong> Reviewed Google Ads and organic search, then documented campaign architecture, budget options, tracking checks, and a prioritized search improvement plan.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">02</span>
    <div><strong>Worked on catalog and campaign coverage.</strong> Resolved a duplicate product ID issue in the merchant feed and launched Shopping campaigns for additional markets.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">03</span>
    <div><strong>Improved search foundations.</strong> Prepared structured-data templates for blog and collection pages, addressed duplicate account-link URLs, and deployed product review widgets.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">04</span>
    <div><strong>Checked the numbers across platforms.</strong> Compared Shopify and Meta purchase reporting and investigated attribution differences rather than treating platform-reported sales as incremental revenue.</div>
  </div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="cs-results-block">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">The results — before and after</h2>
<p>Average monthly Shopify digital total sales were $88,418.30 in January–April 2026 and $184,546.08 in June–September 2026, an increase of 108.7%. May is excluded because work started during the month.</p>
