<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">The situation</h2>
<p>An apparel brand doing $2M+ in annual revenue came to us after 18 months of flat Meta performance. They were spending $100k over 90 days and breaking even at best. ROAS had been hovering around 1.4× for two quarters. Their agency told them the account was "optimized." It wasn't.</p>
</div>

<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">What we found in the audit</h2>
<ul class="cs-list">
  <li>67% of spend was concentrated in 2 ad sets that hadn't generated a profitable sale in 45 days</li>
  <li>Audience overlap between retargeting and prospecting campaigns was above 60% — they were paying to show the same ad to the same people twice</li>
  <li>No creative testing framework — the same 3 static images had been running for 4 months</li>
  <li>Landing page mismatch: ads promised "free shipping on orders over $75," landing page had no mention of the offer</li>
  <li>Conversion tracking double-counting view-through and click-through — reported ROAS was 1.4×, actual was closer to 0.9×</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">What we did</h2>
<div class="cs-steps">
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">01</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Killed the waste.</strong> Paused the two underperforming ad sets immediately. Reallocated that budget to three new audience segments: high-intent purchasers (30-day website visitors, add-to-cart not purchased), lookalikes from top 5% customer LTV list, and interest-based prospecting with tighter demographic filters.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">02</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Rebuilt the funnel structure.</strong> Separated campaigns into TOF (prospecting), MOF (engagement), and BOF (retargeting) with distinct budgets, objectives, and creative. No audience overlap above 15%.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">03</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Introduced UGC creative testing framework.</strong> Sourced 12 UGC video clips from existing customers. Built a 3:2:1 testing ratio per ad set — 3 video variants, 2 static image variants, 1 carousel. Rotated creative every 14 days based on CPA performance.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">04</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Fixed tracking and landing page.</strong> Rebuilt the Meta pixel with server-side events via Conversions API — eliminated double-counting. Updated landing pages to match ad copy promises with consistent offer messaging.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">05</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Weekly optimization cadence.</strong> Bid adjustments every Tuesday. Creative performance review every two weeks. Audience refresh monthly. No week without a documented change.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
</div>
