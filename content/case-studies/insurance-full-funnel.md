<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">The situation</h2>
<p>A commercial insurance broker in the Northeast was spending $12k/month across Meta and Google and generating 18 qualified leads per month at a CPL of $667. They'd been running the same campaigns for 14 months. Performance had plateaued, and the broker assumed the market was just "getting harder." The market wasn't the problem.</p>
</div>

<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">What we found in the audit</h2>
<ul class="cs-list">
  <li>$8k/month going to Google, $4k to Meta — but the data showed Meta CPL was 60% lower for equivalent lead quality</li>
  <li>Google campaigns targeting broad insurance keywords with massive competition and no negative keyword list</li>
  <li>Meta audience was a single interest-based set — no lookalikes, no CRM-based targeting</li>
  <li>Landing page had 9 form fields including "Best time to call" and "How did you hear about us" — both before any trust was established</li>
  <li>Zero retargeting — every visitor who didn't convert on the first visit was gone forever</li>
  <li>No social proof on the landing page — no reviews, no credentials, no trust indicators</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="cs-section">
<h2 class="cs-section-title">What we did</h2>
<div class="cs-steps">
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">01</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Flipped the budget allocation.</strong> Moved $5k from Google to Meta based on CPL data — new split was $3k Google, $9k Meta. Reduced Google spend to branded and high-intent transactional terms only ("commercial liability insurance quote").
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">02</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Rebuilt Meta audience architecture.</strong> Uploaded 3 years of CRM data (past policyholders). Built lookalike audiences at 1%, 2%, and 5% from the highest-LTV customer segment. Layered income and industry targeting on top. Removed all broad interest-based targeting.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">03</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Landing page overhaul.</strong> Cut form fields from 9 to 4 (name, email, phone, coverage type). Added BBB badge, 200+ Google review count, and three client testimonials above the fold. Wrote new headline: "Get your commercial insurance quote in 24 hours" — clear, specific, no jargon.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">04</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Built a 3-stage retargeting sequence.</strong> Stage 1 (days 1–7): awareness video showing the broker's 20-year track record. Stage 2 (days 8–30): educational content about what commercial coverage actually covers. Stage 3 (days 31–60): direct quote offer with urgency. Each stage had unique creative — no repeated ads.
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-do-step">
    <span class="cs-do-num">05</span>
    <div>
      <strong>Lead quality tracking.</strong> Worked with the broker's CRM to tag lead source at the deal level. Within 60 days we could see which audiences produced leads that actually closed — and doubled down on those.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
</div>
