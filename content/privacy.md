<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
<span class="legal-updated">Last updated: July 16, 2026</span>

<p>This Privacy Policy explains how <strong>sem1co1on</strong> ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you visit our website or contact us. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.</p>

<h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>

<p>When you submit our contact form or book a call, we collect the information you choose to give us — typically your name, email address, company, and any message content. You are not required to provide this information, but we need it to respond to your enquiry.</p>

<p>When you visit our site we automatically collect certain technical data through cookies and similar technologies, including your IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and how you interact with our content.</p>

<h2>2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies</h2>
<p>We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Analytics.</strong> We use analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. These collect aggregated usage data such as pages viewed and session duration.</li>
  <li><strong>Marketing and advertising.</strong> We use marketing pixels (such as the Meta Pixel and Google Ads tags) to measure the performance of our campaigns and to show relevant ads to people who have visited our site.</li>
</ul>
<p>You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how the site functions. Where required by law, we will request your consent before setting non-essential cookies.</p>

<h2>3. How We Use Your Information</h2>
<ul>

  <li>To operate, maintain, and improve our website.</li>
  <li>To measure and improve our marketing and advertising performance.</li>
  <li>To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.</li>
</ul>

<p>We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:</p>
